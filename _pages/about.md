## About Me

I am a tenure-track associate professor in the [School of Data Science and Engineering, East China Normal University](https://dase.ecnu.edu.cn/). Before joining ECNU, I was a postdoctoral researcher in the Department of Computer Science, University of Helsinki (work with Assoc. Prof. Michael Mathioudakis). My research interest lies in both the theoretical and practical aspects of data management, data mining, and machine learning.  In particular, my recent studies focus on data stream algorithms, data management for machine learning, algorithmic fairness, and graph mining.

## Education

- Ph.D. in Computer Science, National University of Singapore, Singapore, 2016-2020 (supervisor: Prof. Kian-Lee Tan)
- M.Eng. in Computer Science, Renmin University of China, Beijing, China, 2014 (supervisor: Prof. Shan Wang)
- B.Eng. in Computer Science, Shandong University, Jinan, China, 2011

## Working

- Associate Professor, School of Data Science and Engineering, East China Normal University, 2021-Now
- Postdoc Researcher, Department of Computer Science, University of Helsinki, 2020-2021

## Research Interests

- **Theory:** approximation algorithm; streaming algorithm; submodular optimization; computational geometry; graph algorithm; differential privacy
- **Applications:** social network analytics (influence maximization, outlier detection, network effect); graph mining (pattern search, motif mining, community search); fairness and privacy in algorithms; responsible and ethical AI & DB; LLM applications
